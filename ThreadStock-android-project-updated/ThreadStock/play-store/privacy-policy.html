ThreadStock Privacy Policy

Last updated: YOUR-DATE-HERE

ThreadStock ("the app") is a shop stock, sales and credit manager created by Subhan Ali.

What data the app handles

You enter shop information, products, stock, sales, customer and supplier details, expenses and optional PIN codes. All of it is stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts, no server, no advertising and no analytics. The developer cannot see your data.

Data sharing

The app does not send your data anywhere on its own. If you choose to back up, print, share a receipt or save a PDF, the file is handed to the app you pick (for example Google Drive, WhatsApp or a printer). What happens to it after that is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Fingerprint

If you turn on fingerprint unlock, your phone's own biometric system does the matching. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint.

Permissions

The app only asks for the biometric permission, and only to offer fingerprint unlock.

Your control

You can export a backup at any time and erase everything with More → Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. Because data is only on your device, losing the phone without a backup means losing the data.

Children

The app is intended for shop owners and staff and is not directed at children.
