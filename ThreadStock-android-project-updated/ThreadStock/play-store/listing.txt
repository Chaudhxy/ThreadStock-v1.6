----- FULL DESCRIPTION (English) -----
ThreadStock is a simple shop manager for thread, yarn, embroidery and sewing-supply shops.
It works fully offline, so it is fast and always available. Your data stays on your phone.

STOCK
- Products with colours and shade codes, category groups, low-stock reorder list
- Bulk price changes and quick stock-in

SALES
- Cash, Credit (udhaar) and Online sales
- Online payments: Bank Transfer (choose from all Pakistani banks and enter the account number),
  JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay and SadaPay (enter the wallet number)
- See where each online payment was received, with bank and wallet logos
- Filter sales by All, Cash, Credit and Online
- Returns and exchanges

RECEIPTS
- Professional invoice with your logo, shop name, address and phone, date and time
- Print, save as PDF or image, share on WhatsApp

CUSTOMERS AND SUPPLIERS
- Customer credit ledger, due dates, payments received
- Supplier balances

REPORTS
- Daily and monthly sales, cash, credit and online totals, profit, expenses

SAFETY
- Owner PIN, staff PINs and fingerprint unlock
- Backup to Google Drive or your phone, restore at any time

LANGUAGES: English and Urdu (right-to-left), light and dark mode.
